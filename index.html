Host Speaker

Apostle David E. Taylor

Author, Speaker, Advocate for Faith and Healing

Apostle David E. Taylor is the founder of Joshua Media Ministries International (JMMI), devoted to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ with a focus on healing, deliverance, and face-to-face visitations from the Lord.

For over 36 years, he has faithfully prayed for the sick, proclaiming that Jesus still heals today.

Since 2008, Apostle Taylor has hosted the Miracle Crusade Against Cancer right here in Taylor, Michigan, where thousands have testified to God’s miraculous power through healings and deliverances.

Mission

"To bring hope to the hopeless, healing to the sick, and miracles to those who believe. Together, we can make a difference in the fight against cancer."

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Miracle Crusade Against Cancer?

The Miracle Crusade Against Cancer is a free, multi-day healing event with Apostle David E. Taylor, focused on praying for the sick in Jesus’ name.

When is it happening?

July 28 – August 1, 2026. Services are held daily at 10:00 AM and 7:00 PM.

Where is it located?

20320 Superior Rd, Taylor, MI 48180, in the Detroit area.

How much does it cost?

The Crusade is completely free and open to everyone.

Do I need to register?

Walk-ins are welcome. However, we recommend calling 1-877-843-4567 if you need hotel discounts, travel assistance, or additional details.

Are there hotel discounts?

Yes. We’ve partnered with local hotels to offer discounted rates. Call 1-877-843-4567 for booking assistance and discount codes.

What happens at the Crusade?

Each service includes worship, teaching about Jesus’ healing power, prayer for the sick, real-life testimonies, and a welcoming atmosphere of faith.

Is this only for people with cancer?

No. We pray for all sicknesses and needs. Anyone seeking healing, hope, or a touch from God is welcome.

Can I watch online?

Yes. The Crusade will be streamed live on Zoom, social media, and major Christian networks worldwide. Visit joshuamediaministries.org/cac2026 for details.

Is prayer available?

Yes. Prayer is available during and after services, and ongoing mentorship and prayer support are also offered.

Will there be childcare?

Childcare maybe provided for the evening services. Please call 1-877-843-4567 for more Information.

How can I get more information?

Call 1-877-843-4567 or visit joshuamediaministries.org/cac2026.
